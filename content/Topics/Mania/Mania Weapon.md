**Mania Weapons** as a general term can refer to any weapon utilising mania, like the [[Rancor]]. But [[Parma]] developed a way to mass produce powerful mania weapons utilising [[Mania Crystal]]s harvested by torturing Sinners.

The design is based on [[Summer]]s [[Ring Weapon]] research at the [[Ring Research Institute]], which was leaked to Parma in N.F. 111 by Laplace.

But the raw materials for producing them were sourced by the Underground from N.F. 103 to 108.
