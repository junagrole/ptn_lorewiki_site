While slowly advancing into the gang occupied building complex [[Earl]] gives Chief a short history lesson about the [[Syndicate|West District]]. The conversation is cut short by a contamination trap.

The trap was not set by Sinners, but by [[Thou Voodoo]] gang members wielding [[Mania Weapon]]s. Coordinating with them against [[The Legion]] a group of [[Gonzales]] members flank the MBCC team.

Before reinforcements from the [[Red Falcon]] and [[Carnivore]] can join the fray they get taken out by [[Zoya]] and her Legionaries. To oppose Zoya the rival gangs unite for the first time, but that only gives the Legion the chance for a decisive strike.

Meanwhile [[Hecate]] is mostly worried about the unknown mania weapons the gangsters wield.
